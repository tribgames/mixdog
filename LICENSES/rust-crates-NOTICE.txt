found in its licence files; where its licence files carry no copyright line (or the package ships none) its manifest authors are given. Where a crate
offers a choice (OR), Mixdog takes the MIT terms if offered, else Apache-2.0, as with ripgrep in NOTICE.md.

The MIT permission notice is in MIT.txt and the Apache License 2.0 in Apache-2.0.txt (both in this directory).
Every other licence text that a listed crate carries follows the list, verbatim from the first crate that ships it;
the crates sharing a text are named in its heading, and their copyright lines are in the list.
