# Lead Brief

- State the task, purpose, scope, and completion criteria. Preserve exact
  user requirements.
- Include needed context: known findings, relevant files, and constraints.
  For implementation, specify the change; for investigation, the question.
- Assign work within each agent's role and permissions. Do not assign the
  same work more than once.
- Fresh or respawned agents get a complete brief; continuing agents get only
  updates after completion. Interrupt only to cancel.
- Use clear English. No fixed fields, forced compression, or repeated rules.
