# Reading the audit, the receipt, and the pages

Read when interpreting what `author`, `qa`, or `render` returns, and before
inspecting the rendered pages. Measurements locate possible defects; they do
not prove good line breaks, registration, or visual quality.

## 1. What `author` returns
- **pages** — every slide rendered and one contact sheet of the whole deck (unless `render:false`); the rendered pages come back as `images[]` (`page`, `path`) with the sheet as `contactSheet` (its file is always written; its image is attached past four slides, since a shorter deck's pages already show the sequence), and `pageCount` says how many the deck has; the HTML route adds `render.compare`, the HTML beside the PPTX render per page.
- **the composition receipt** — per slide: charts, tables, pictures, drawn shapes and their presets, fields, text boxes, background role, and `observe` (air, quadrant air, largest object share, visual footprint, text columns and stray boxes, fill areas, largest-type top, visual centroid and its offset from center, and after a render the pixel-read `renderAir`); `deck.rhythm` lists air, largest-type tops, centroid x, and render air in sequence.
- **`audit`** — the measured read (fit, bounds, contrast, spacing, package faults) as `status` pass/fail, per-slide counts, the top targets, and the fix `round`.

## 2. Receipt fields
Observations, not design targets; read the receipt as a diagnostic, not an object quota. `air` canvas share without shape footprints; `quadrantAir` [top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right], `contentAir` the same using content only (sent only where it differs); an empty list is omitted; `fieldFill` content coverage inside fields of at least 6% of the canvas, lowest first. `largestShare` largest object share; `visualShare` non-text footprint; `presence` the largest carrier's share (chart, table, picture, group, or contour; 0 = type alone); `fills` surface colours by area; `textColumns` alignment groups and unmatched edges (`labels` = small text bound to a contour or connector, kept out of the columns and gaps). `largestTextTop`, `bodyTop` positions in inches, not shared baselines; `bodyFill` the span of content under the largest type relative to the lower safe margin. `centroid` area-weighted [x, y] in canvas fractions; `centroidOffset` its distance from centre normalized by 0.05 horizontally and 0.15 vertically. `renderAir` pixels without local variation; `renderBalance` centered, leftRight, topBottom and their mean (higher = more even, not better). `gaps` vertical gaps in 0.05-inch steps; `typeSet`, `textColors` observed sizes and colours. `chars` authored characters per slide (reference body pages carry 330-900; under 120 with no carrier is a claim or a hollow); `grammar` beat | evidence | text, with `deck.shape` their shares. `renderLargest` the page share of the biggest connected object after a render (reference decks 0.21-0.71, median 0.4). After a render `deck.rhythm.colour` is each page's colourfulness and `deck.pacing` reads it: `colourSpread` (frontier decks 16-36, one template 5-8), `longestQuiet` (consecutive pages under the median with no beat), `quietAnchors` (anchor lines that rendered quiet). `specs` the kit's spec carriers read from their signatures — one anatomy per carrier is the spec kept, two is an override to explain. `absent` lists unused families, not required objects: check an inferred missing carrier against the message, since a faithful alternative may carry it. A slide or deck marked `unchanged: true` matches the receipt the session sent last; only its render readings are new.

## 3. Preview fidelity
The portable preview is LibreOffice, and it spaces Hangul and CJK *chart category labels* by character — `1분기` is drawn as `1 분기` while the chart part holds the unspaced string, and PowerPoint draws it unspaced. Check the string (`source-extract.mjs` or the chart's `c:v` values) before treating it as a defect; slide text is unaffected.

## 4. Inspection catalog
"Manual" means only the rendered image shows it.

Hard:
1. Out of bounds: any element past the 13.33 × 7.5 canvas. → `shape_out_of_bounds`, `content_touches_page_edge`
2. Overflow: text past its container or clipped at a box edge. → `text_overflow`, `text_clipped`, `text_box_too_narrow`
3. Text overlap: two text blocks intersect; text through a shape or line. → `shape_overlap` (text through a line → manual)
4. Readability: contrast below 4.5:1 (3:1 for ≥ 18 pt or bold ≥ 14 pt); text on a picture without a scrim. → `low_contrast`, `low_visual_contrast` (scrim presence → manual)
5. Collision: shapes overlap where z-order breaks the meaning — a band over a chart's category axis, a source line running into the table, or a picture or filled shape added after a text box and covering the words behind it (a card drawn *under* its own text is how a slide is built and is not one). → `shape_overlap`
6. Broken picture: a raster that draws nothing, stretched, or enlarged past its own pixels. → `blank_image`, `image_aspect_distorted`, `image_low_resolution`
7. Missing element: leftover placeholder copy or a tool's citation marker (`【4:0†source】`, `citeturn0search3`) where the source belongs; a figure with no fact. → `placeholder_text`, `unfilled_token`, `number_without_fact`
8. Font compatibility: missing or substituted fonts that change visible fit require repair or disclosure. Color ladders, hue counts, and family counts are style diagnostics, not defects by themselves; an approved brand can override their defaults. → `unsafe_font_family`, `accent_hue_overuse`, `font_family_overuse`, `theme_background_drift`
9. Editability: a chart drawn from rectangles; a chart whose embedded workbook is gone, so Edit Data opens nothing; a confirmed paragraph split into single-line boxes. → `dead_vector_chart`, `chart_data_unlinked`; `text_fragmentation` is an advisory candidate, not proof that independent labels should be merged.

Advisory (information the runtime reports; the author decides, never a polish target): monotony readings such as the same composition on most slides, one background throughout, or few visual roles (`repeated_layout_grammar`, `repeated_render_composition`, `flat_visual_rhythm`, `visual_role_variety_low`, `card_grid_overuse`), a canvas the reviewer reads as inactive (`under_composed_slide`, `slide_visual_density_low`, `meaningful_visual_missing`; `title_line_hollow` for a title over an empty middle and one line at the foot — a statement sets its claim large instead), and the plan read-back (`plan_count_mismatch`, `art_direction_candidates_missing`; `plan_promise_missing` is not advisory — `author` refuses on it, `SKILL.md` §3). A deliberate composition overrides all of them; the receipt is where the author answers them.

Soft (fix when the change is local):
- Line step under 1.05× the font size, or an empty band over 1.5 in between the slide's content — the footer line and page number count, so a body that stops halfway is measured, not hidden by them (breathing and statement slides exempt). Extend a region into the band or move the lower block up. → `text_spacing_tight`, `vertical_imbalance`
- An element 1-6 pt off an axis its neighbours already share (a spine, a column edge, a row of tops): snap it to the axis with the kit's `mid()` / `band()`, or move it clear so the offset reads as a decision. → `axis_drift`; a row of equal cards whose gaps differ by more than 5% (a wobble, not a break between groups) → `peer_gap_uneven`
- The most prominent element is not what the slide is about. → `emphasis_mismatch`
- Caption more than 0.5 in from its picture; margins under 0.5 in; blocks closer than 0.3 in. → `stat_label_detached`, `edge_margin`, `shapes_too_close`
- A row of peers set in different type: the page's own geometry says which boxes form a row (the snapshot reports them as `column-title-1`, `metric-value-2`, `step-title-3`), and one of them at another size or face reads as a mistake rather than as emphasis. Set the row in one size and face, or make the odd one deliberate by changing what it says. → `peer_style_inconsistent`
- A page of prose with no carrier: past 900 characters and covering nearly half the canvas, the slide is a document being projected, and shrinking the type only hides it. Split the page or cut it to its claim and its evidence. → `slide_text_dense`
- A page of three or more text boxes all set in one size, with no carrier and nothing leading them: the eye has no place to start. Set the page's claim above the rest of the type, or let a chart, table, or picture carry it. → `slide_hierarchy_flat`
- Two or more series told apart by colour alone: the chart draws no legend, its labels carry no series name, and the page never names them either, so the colours stand for something the reader cannot attribute. Turn the legend on, label the series, or write the names beside the chart (a legend the page writes counts). → `chart_series_unnamed`

## 5. Page QC report
`qc-pages.mjs` writes `pages[].reason` per page: improved, polished as unchanged, clean, scope, regression, audit_failed, execution_failed. `--no-vision` runs the geometry read only (a text-only model, or no renderer); at most 20 pages per run.
